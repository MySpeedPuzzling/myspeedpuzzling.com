# Competition toolkit

Loaded automatically when you read or edit files of the competition toolkit (round formats, the reveal at the stopwatch start, standings, advancement steps and the bracket, QR tags + competitor pages + time proposals, the offline judge page, displays and overlays). The rest of competition management is `.claude/rules/features/events.md`. Each entry is the as-built summary; its doc under `docs/features/competitions-management/` is the design of record - read it before changing behaviour, and update both when behaviour changes.

- **Toolkit frame**: `docs/features/competitions-management/README.md` "Competition toolkit" (+ `toolkit/STATUS.md` = the living delivery record, `toolkit/R-review-and-plan.md` = the binding plan, A/B/C/D design docs, `00-context.md` = Jan's request verbatim) — **nothing is a new default**: every part is opt-in per event or per round, an untouched competition renders byte-identically and runs the same statements (goldens `RoundResultsStateGoldenTest`, `OfficialRoundResultsGoldenTest`, `CompetitionNameTagsGoldenTest`, `RecordRoundResultsStatementPinTest`, `DetailPagesQueryBudgetTest`; keep them unchanged unless a test pins something you only *add* behind a setting). Settings and editors behind `COMPETITION_TOOLKIT_MANAGE` (`CompetitionToolkitVoter` = exactly `COMPETITION_EDIT`: every organiser of the event, admins - no feature flag, retired 2026-11-17); what was switched on works for everybody. Hub `competition_toolkit` (`/en/manage-event-toolkit/{id}`, one card per area). Every surface that shows a round's puzzles reads `RoundPuzzlesVisibleNow` - the public rule, organisers and referees included, labels instead of names before the reveal (an early reveal would be a disaster); public official entries only through `OfficialEntrantsView` (published rounds, hidden players dropped without renumbering, private players masked). Every new surface goes into the canaries (`BlocklistCanaryTest`, `PrivateProfileCanaryTest`, `DraftCanaryTest`, `SecretPuzzleRoutesTest`) or their documented exemption list. Leftovers: `docs/TODO.md` "Competition toolkit v2".
