## 1. Why

- On phones (where most page views and most signed-in sessions happen) every section sits behind ☰, the header shows six unlabelled icons, the account menu ("My profile") holds 15 links, and the bell count is noise (almost every unread notification is "a favorite added a time").
- A large share of all feedback touches navigation, and part of it says outright "I can't find X" (much of that from organisers).
- The player profile mixes four ideas and renders every result unpaged (the biggest profiles are large enough that the page grows too heavy for a phone; filters are lost on Back).
- FAQ and Stopwatch hold main-menu places with very low use, while notifications, the library and search (used in many signed-in sessions) do not.
- The long tail has no home on phones: without one complete index about 12 destinations would be reachable only from the footer and about 25 only from one deep page. Desktop carries most of that long tail (moderation, organisers, marketplace, players).
- Evidence: hidden navigation is used about half as often as visible navigation (NN/g 2016); labelled tabs beat icon-only; 3–5 labelled destinations on a phone bar (Material 3); a visible search field gets used far more (NN/g, Baymard).

**Place mapping (active state)** — one resolver maps every route to a place: puzzle pages, brands, pieces pages, marketplace, puzzle picker, and **your own library and its list pages** → Puzzles; your other own player pages (Overview, Results, Statistics, Calendar, Achievements, Favorites, XP/karma history, pairs & teams, settings) → Profile; other players' pages and team pages → Community; ladders, players, comparison, events, organizations, series/editions/events → Community; hub, recent activity → Home; add form, stopwatch, multiscan, recap → Add; inbox, help, the `menu` fallback page → no place. The resolver also names the Menu row to highlight (`aria-current`) in the panel and the docked column.

## 4. Surfaces

### 4.1 Phone top bar
- 52 px, solid white, hairline; soft shadow once scrolled; stays on screen while the page scrolls (owner, 2026-10-16 - it slid away on scroll down until then, §12 "Desktop density"). On viewports < 480 px tall it scrolls with the page (WCAG technique C34).
- Contents, left to right: **logo** (→ Home; always visible: 36 px mark, 28 px below 320 px) · **search field** (`type="search"`, 16 px text, one-word placeholder per locale: Search, Hledat, Suchen, Buscar, **Chercher**, 検索 — "Recherche" (81 px) does not fit the 74 px text room at 320 px, "Chercher" (69 px) does; the native-speaker check stays an owner step; scan button inside from 360 px, inside the opened search view at 320–359 px; opens global search) · **?** · **Inbox** (signed-in players) · **☰** (last, at the far right edge; 44 px button, accessible name "Menu", `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded`; a dot only for moderators and admins while one of their queues holds items, §4.12 "Menu data"). **All top-bar tools sit 4 px apart** (one gap token; no larger gap before ☰). Measured field with 4 px gaps: ≈ 158 px at 360, ≈ 190 px at 393, ≈ 118 px at 320.
- Running stopwatch: the pill takes the search field's place and search becomes an icon — logo · ⏱ · 🔍 · ? · Inbox · ☰; below 360 px the pill shows mm:ss and is ≤ 70 px wide.
- In an installed app a back button is added before the logo on non-root pages (root pages: hub, homepage, puzzles, own library, add, community, own profile); below 390 px the search field then becomes a search icon. Back button **and** a running stopwatch below 393 px: the search icon gives way first (search stays reachable in the Menu panel's field); the logo never gives way.
- **Installed-app state** is read in one place: `matchMedia('(display-mode: standalone)').matches || navigator.standalone` sets `html.is-standalone` (inline head script, before first paint). Standalone-only markup (the back button, the Help sheet's "Reload page") is server-rendered with class `standalone-only` and shown only under `html.is-standalone`, so server tests assert the markup and the class and the browser decides visibility. Test hook: only when `navigator.webdriver === true`, localStorage `msp:test-standalone` forces the state.
- The four tools carry stable action ids for a later native app (§4.14): `data-bar-action="search|help|inbox|menu"`.
- Replaces the old topbar band (admin key, feedback, membership, feature requests, language), the old hamburger and the toolbar icons. The new ☰ stays where the old one was (far right), so "Everything is in ☰" is the launch message.

### 4.4 Inbox
- For you = the `notifications` route; Messages = `conversations_list` (its own sub-tabs stay). Both show one Inbox header with the two tabs, each with its own unread count.
- The Inbox link (top bar, Menu row) points at the tab holding the unread items: Messages when only messages or message requests are unread, For you otherwise (decided from the badge statement's parts, no extra query). The badge's accessible name says what is new ("Inbox, 2 new messages").
- Other ways into a conversation stay: Message on profiles and the player card, marketplace chat links.
- **Badge (D3)** in one statement replacing today's two counts, as two parts: **For you** = unread notifications except `SubscribedPlayerAddedTime` and `NewConversationRequest`; **Messages** = unread accepted conversations + pending message requests to the viewer. Badge = For you + Messages; each tab shows its own part.
- **Several instances, one update:** the badge appears in the phone top bar, the desktop top bar and the Menu's Inbox row (phone panel). Every instance carries class `inbox-badge`; the realtime Mercure push (`MercureNotifier::notifyInboxBadgeChanged()` and its callers, rendered without `app.request`) is one Turbo Stream that reaches every instance (custom action `inbox_badge` in `assets/turbo-stream-actions.js`, `targets=".inbox-badge"`). It carries **numbers only** - `for-you`, `conversations`, `requests` and `at` (when they were read) - because a push runs without a request and so without the page's locale; `assets/inbox_badge.js` sets the number, the enclosing Inbox link's target tab (`href`) and its accessible name from them, with the texts and URLs the page's own Inbox link carries (§12).
- The notifications page passes the parts it knows (For you = 0, because it has just marked everything read) and never a total of 0, so unread messages stay visible there.
- 30-day pointer in For you: "Your favorites' new times are now on Home › Favorites".

### 4.5 Help & feedback
- Public route `help` (6 locales, `noindex, follow`) = full-page fallback. **The sheet is the dynamic modal's content**: the ?/Feedback buttons, the Menu's "Send feedback" row and the end-of-page line are links to `help` (with `?url=` of the current page) targeting `modal-frame`; the content root carries `data-modal-sheet data-modal-history` and the G2 sheet controller. For a `Turbo-Frame: modal-frame` request `help` answers only its frame (no layout, no Menu; `Vary: Turbo-Frame`, never shared-cached), otherwise the full page. So `FeedbackController` (which answers in-sheet only for `modal-frame` requests) and the HELP-05 fix stay on one path. Signed-in: the feedback form posts to `feedback` with an explicit `action`. Links carrying `?url=` are `rel="nofollow"`.
- Order: feedback box (signed-in) · "Suggest a correction to this puzzle" (puzzle pages, first) · Ideas & feature requests · FAQ · Getting started · How progress works (XP visible) · Where did it go? · Language · Reload page (installed app only — `standalone-only`, §4.1 —, the non-drag twin of pull-to-refresh). ### 4.13 Gestures and platform feel (D22)
Principle: don't invent gestures; make the ones phones already teach work well, make every tap answer at once, and give each platform its own feedback only where the platforms really differ **and** MSP draws the moment itself. Everything structural stays identical.

**Platform attribute.** An inline ES5 script in `<head>` (before the stylesheet link; in `base.html.twig` and every layout that does not extend it) sets `<html data-os="ios|android|other">` before first paint, in this order: the native-app user-agent markers (the same as `PlatformDetector`) → UA-CH `navigator.userAgentData.platform` → `Android` in the user agent → iOS by user agent, then the iPadOS desktop user agent (`Macintosh` + `navigator.maxTouchPoints > 1`) → `CSS.supports('-webkit-touch-callout', 'none')` (true only in iOS WebKit); anything else is `other` (desktop, Chrome's "desktop site" mode, unknown). Client-only: never sent to the server, never stored, so guest HTML stays byte-identical across devices and shared-cacheable. `assets/platform.js` `os()` is the only JS reader; CSS uses `[data-os="…"]`. **It is not the native-app flag:** `data-native-app` (§4.14, server-rendered) and the existing `platform-ios` / `platform-android` / `native-app` classes from `app.js` mean "inside the Hotwire Native app" — a browser on an iPhone is `data-os="ios"` and `platform-web`. Feature checks stay feature checks (`navigator.vibrate`, `document.startViewTransition`). `(pointer: coarse)` stays a sizing signal, never a platform signal.

**What differs per platform** (desktop/other gets neither skin):

| | iOS | Android | Desktop / other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pressed feedback (G4, G11) | 10 % state layer, no ripple | state layer + ripple from the touch point | state layer on `:active`, hover as today |
| Haptics (G10) | only real `switch` toggles (the system's own haptic) | `haptic()` patterns, per-device Vibration switch | none |
| Place switch (G12) | instant | fade-through, 300 ms | instant |
| Installed-app pull indicator (G17) | bare spinner | Material disc, 40 px | — |
| Long-press label (G16) | bubble, no haptic | bubble + `longPress` | native `title` |

Identical everywhere: IA, bars, the Menu panel (§4.12), sheets (handle 36 × 5 px, top radius 16 px, scrim, × always, G2 thresholds), dialogs (G13), Rubik, the flatpickr date pickers, Back (never animated by MSP), overscroll and the browsers' own pull-to-refresh in tabs, tapping the active tab (G3), the thumbnail morph on both phones (G15).

  | Name | Pattern | Fires on |
  |---|---|---|

- **G12 Place switches** (GEST-12): Android fades through (old out in 90 ms, emphasized accelerate; new in over 210 ms after 90 ms with a .92 → 1 scale, emphasized decelerate); the bars, rail and docked column stand still; iOS and desktop switch instantly; never on Back or restores, never with reduced motion. Mechanics: `<meta name="view-transition" content="same-origin">` is always rendered and set to `none` in JS unless `data-os="android"` (or a G15 morph) and outside the native app; `html[data-vt="place"]` only for place links (`[data-nav-place]`); every other view transition has no animation. As built (`assets/view_transition_gate.js`, which reads the live head meta at render - Turbo 8.0.23): the bars carry a `view-transition-name` only while `html[data-vt]` is set, and the top bar none while the phone search is open (`html[data-search-open]`) - the open search panel leaves with the page instead of freezing over it; Back never animates (restore visits and popstates the browser animates itself set the meta to `none`).
- **G13 Confirmations are in-page dialogs** (GEST-13): `Turbo.config.forms.confirm` and a `confirmDialog(message)` helper open MSP's confirm `<dialog>` (Cancel left, action right, danger style on request; Esc and scrim cancel; focus returns to the trigger; default button labels in 6 locales). Every inline `return confirm(…)` in player- and organiser-facing templates becomes `data-turbo-confirm`; every controller `window.confirm` becomes `await confirmDialog(…)`; `/admin` may keep native confirms (listed). Reason: iOS Safari silently refuses native dialogs after a Back to a `pushState` entry (hotwired/turbo#637), and G1 creates exactly those entries. A watchdog in `app.js` wraps `window.confirm` and, on `data-os="ios"`, sends one `dialog_suppressed` P0-03 event per page view when it returns `false` within 50 ms.
- **G14 Swipe between Home's two feeds** (GEST-16), phones (< 768 px) only: both panes in one horizontal scroll-snap scroller (`scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`, `scroll-snap-stop: always`, `overscroll-behavior-x: contain`, no JS gesture code); the tabs stay the main control (a tap scrolls the pane into view); the selected tab follows the settled pane (`scrollend`; without it the IntersectionObserver at ≥ 60 % visible only selects the tab, and the feed settles 150 ms after the scroller stopped **with no finger on it** - touchstart/touchend/touchcancel tracked); the indicator follows `scrollLeft`; the scroller's height follows the visible pane (a height is set only during a swipe, none at rest); before the lazy controller connects, the phone tabs behave like Bootstrap tabs (only `.active` shows - nothing shifts when it wakes); below 768 px the scroller reaches the page edges and the panes carry the side padding (tables edge to edge, shadows inside from 576 px); the hidden pane is `inert` and its Live feed does not poll (the `live_refresh` "shown" check uses an IntersectionObserver). Identical on both platforms; desktop keeps the tabs. The Hub rework is a separate project: keep the change local to the feed switcher.
- **G15 Thumbnail → puzzle page morph** (GEST-17) on Android and iOS from catalogue, library and search cards (`[data-morph-source]`; not table rows): the tapped thumbnail and the puzzle page's box image share `view-transition-name: puzzle-hero` (group 300 ms emphasized decelerate, `object-fit: cover`, root cross-fade 150 ms). Skipped when the page arrives > 400 ms after the tap, when the thumbnail is not decoded, on desktop, on Back and restores, with reduced motion; the name is cleared on `turbo:load`. Never clear `puzzle-hero` or close the phone search view in `turbo:before-cache`: Turbo fires it before the view transition captures the old page (the morph also needs the search view still on screen at that capture).
- **G16 Long-press a truncated tab** (GEST-18), only when its label is not fully shown (icon-only fallback, or `scrollWidth > clientWidth`): hold 500 ms without moving > 10 px → a bubble with the full label 12 px above the tab (`role="tooltip"`, the tab's `aria-describedby`); Android also `haptic('longPress')`; the release does not navigate; it hides on release or after 1.5 s; a shorter tap navigates; tab links (only they) get `-webkit-touch-callout: none` and a prevented `contextmenu`.
- **G17 Installed-app pull indicator** (GEST-19): Android and other the Material disc, 40 px, arrow → spinner (today's look, resized); iOS a bare spinner that fades and rotates in with the pull, no disc, no arrow. G6's thresholds unchanged.
- **Don't**: swipe between places, player tabs, chips or Inbox tabs; custom long-press menus (G16 is a label, not a menu); swipe actions on rows; double-tap actions; an edge swipe to open the Menu; an own pull-to-refresh in browser tabs; blocking zoom; hiding the bottom bar; haptics on navigation, or iOS haptic tricks; animating Back ourselves; an iOS push slide or an Android shared-axis transition into object pages (they would start only after the server answers); a per-platform font, native date pickers or imitated iOS 26 glass sheets; gesture tutorials.

### 4.14 Native-ready navigation (a Hotwire Native app later)
MySpeedPuzzling plans a Hotwire Native hybrid app later. Symfony UX Native is experimental and is **not** installed now (do not add it). `src/Services/PlatformDetector.php` already recognises the app by its user agent (Twig `is_native_app()`, `is_web()`, `is_ios()`, `is_android()`); `assets/app.js` mirrors it (`window.isNativeApp`, `html.native-app`). This release keeps every navigation piece ready for the app without building any of it:
- **(a) Every layer is also a plain route** that renders the same content as a full page: Menu `menu`, Help `help`, Inbox tabs `notifications` / `conversations_list`, player card `player_card`, achievement sheet `achievement_detail`. Openers inside page content are `<a href>` to that route, enhanced by JS on the web, so a native path configuration can present them as native screens or modals.
- **(b) One flag:** `<html data-native-app="ios|android">`, rendered from `is_native_app()` (absent on the web). One JS module `assets/platform.js` reads it (`isNativeApp()`, `nativePlatform()` → `'ios' | 'android' | null`) next to `os()` (the device OS from `data-os`, §4.13 — a different thing: an iPhone browser is `os() === 'ios'` and not native); `app.js`'s existing globals keep working and agree with it. Every web-only navigation behaviour checks it and steps aside inside the app: G1 history entries, our pull-to-refresh (G6, G17), the web top bar, tab bar, rail, docked column and Menu panel (§4.10 — the app brings its own), edge-swipe handling, view transitions (G12, G15), the instant-Back restore handling (G9), haptics through `navigator.vibrate`. The detector also accepts the `Hotwire Native iOS` / `Hotwire Native Android` markers (today's Hotwire Native user agents still contain `Turbo Native …`), in PHP, `app.js` and the `data-os` head script alike.
- **(c) Haptics through one helper:** `haptic(name)` in `assets/haptics.js` (names, patterns and rules: §4.13 G10; the online round's `signal()` shares the backend) with a pluggable backend chosen in one place — `navigator.vibrate` on the web (Android only), a Hotwire Native bridge component later (UIKit feedback generators / Android `performHapticFeedback`), none inside the app until that exists; later also `navigator.playHaptics` when the Web Haptics proposal ships. Every `navigator.vibrate` call in `assets/` goes through it.
- **(d) Stable action ids:** the top-bar tools carry `data-bar-action="search|help|inbox|menu"` (ids from one PHP enum), next to their accessible name, their URL (the plain route) and, on Inbox, the badge count, so a later bridge component can turn them into native buttons.
- **Caching:** HTML rendered for the app differs from web HTML at the same URL, so responses to app requests are never marked shared-cacheable (`AnonymousCacheHeadersSubscriber` skips them).
- Out of scope: the app, its native tab bar, path configuration and bridge components (incl. the haptics bridge).

## 7. Placement rulebook
Before adding any page or link write "A player wants to …". Ask in order; the first yes decides:
1. Action on a thing that has a page → a button on that page (or its ⋯). No nav entry.
2. Page of one object → no nav entry; reached from lists, search, notifications; breadcrumb to its parent.
3. Only for a role → Menu › Your roles (holders only) + the role's button where it acts.
4. Creates/records something → the Add page (+ a create button where the thing lives).
5. Needed on every page and a universal finder, help, or a live state you didn't cause → the top bar (fixed set: search, Help & feedback, Inbox, ☰ Menu).
6. Configures your account → Settings (and a search-only entry).
7. Explains, helps, collects feedback, or is about MSP → Help & feedback (legal/about: footer + Menu › About).
8. About one player → others may see it: a player-page tab; owner manages own things: Menu › Your puzzling / Your puzzles / Account; owner-only view of own data: an owner-only tab, also a Menu row.
9. Shared catalogue/community content → its noun decides: a puzzle → Puzzles; people, events, rankings → Community.
10. Claimed top task → the top-level test: a destination; ≥ 20 % of weekly active players every week for 8 weeks in its normal home and above the weakest place; a tree test with ≥ 50 players; replaces one place; announced, old entry redirected.

Rules: five places, never six · places only in bars (Add opens a page) · fixed top-bar tools · one home per page + ≤ 2 other entries (the Menu, the footer and search are indexes and do not count) · **every destination is a Menu row or a search-only entry** (guard test) · Menu groups ordered by use and then fixed; the order is re-decided only from measured data, never per player · navigation is not a launch channel (one expiring nudge instead) · "New" labels only on new pages, gone after first visit or 14 days, never on places · two levels under a place · labels are 1–2-word nouns checked in 6 locales at 320 px · role areas only for holders · registry `NavigationRegistry` (mirrored in `docs/navigation.md`) + guard tests · every nav link has `data-nav="surface/place/item"` and is measured; quarterly review · every layer is also a plain route (§4.14).

Planned pages (examples): Duels → "Challenge" on the card/header and puzzle pages, each duel an object page, Menu › Your puzzling › Your duels · Insights → owner-only tab beside Statistics and a Menu row, "See why" links on the recap and own puzzle rows · Thanks & resources → footer + Help sheet + Menu › About · Blog → footer Learn + "What's new" in Help + Menu › Help & learn; a new post gets a 14-day Home card · Your week → Menu › Your puzzling · Rating history → player-page tab · Compare → Community › Puzzlers + Menu › Your puzzling.
