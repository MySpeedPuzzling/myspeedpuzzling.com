## Findings

### F1 · minor · `assets/styles/_auth.scss:19`, `:333`, `templates/faq.html.twig:63`
**Problem.** Three scroll margins still carry the old header's height, so they now count the bar twice.
- NAV-11 moved the bar's room to `html { scroll-padding-top: var(--topbar-h) }` and removed `--topbar-h` from the 11 margins that read it.
- These three hard-code the room instead (`6rem` = the old header plus a gap) and were not converted:
  - the auth fields: `.form-control` ("never tucks a field under the sticky header");
  - `.auth-disclosure-panel`;
  - every FAQ question (`style="scroll-margin-top: 6rem;"`).
- Before: 6rem from the viewport top (96 px on phones, 108 px from 1200 px, where the root font is 112.5 %). Now: padding plus margin = 148 px on phones and 172 px at 1440 - a 6rem empty band between the bar and the target.
- `NavLayoutCssTest::testScrollMarginsReadTheLiveTopBarHeight` only looks for `--header-height` / `--topbar-h` in stylesheets, so it misses fixed values and inline styles.

**Reproduction.**
1. At 360, open `/en/faq#duplicate-accounts` (linked from `social_register_confirm.html.twig`). `faq_anchor_controller` opens the question and calls `scrollIntoView({block: 'start'})`.
2. `document.getElementById('duplicate-accounts').getBoundingClientRect().top` is about 148 (172 at 1440). Before `00f5250f` it was 96 (108).

**Fix.**
- Keep only the gap: `scroll-margin-top: 1rem` in both `_auth.scss` rules and on the FAQ item (better as a class than inline).
- Extend the guard to fixed `scroll-margin-top` values of 3rem / 48 px or more in `assets/styles` and to inline `scroll-margin` in `templates/`.

### F2 · minor · `assets/controllers/global_search_controller.js:136-148` (`handleFocus`)
**Problem.** Tab passing through the bar's search field still stops the page's camera.
- `handleFocus` sends `barcode-scan:close` before the new `focusMovedByTab()` test. Every `barcode_scanner_controller` listens to that event on `window` (`:51`) and calls `stopScanning()`.
- NAV-11 made a Tab pass "no change of context" (WCAG 3.2.1) for the search, but the side effect stayed.
- On `/en/multiscan` the scanner starts by itself and scans continuously (`_scanner.html.twig`, `autostart: true`). A desktop keyboard user who goes back up with Shift+Tab passes the field (… avatar → Inbox → ? → + Add → **search** → logo), and the camera turns off although nothing opened.

**Reproduction.** At 1440 as a member with a webcam, open `/en/multiscan` and wait for the camera. Focus a control in `main`, then press Shift+Tab until the logo has focus. The viewfinder is gone (`stopScanning()` ran), and `html[data-search-open]` was never set.

**Fix.** Close the page's scanner when the search opens, not when the field gets the focus:
- Move the bar's `barcode-scan:close` into `openView()`. Today `openView()` sends nothing, so a search opened by typing after a Tab pass leaves the page's camera running.
- In `handleFocus`, return early for a Tab pass in bar mode:

  ```js
  if (!this.isMenu() && focusMovedByTab()) { return; }
  ```

- The Menu field keeps its dispatch on focus.

### F3 · minor · `assets/controllers/global_search_controller.js:136-148` (`handleFocus`), `:481-496` (`restoreFocus`)
**Problem.** A focus that the browser hands back opens the search, as if it were a tap.
- When a window or tab gets the focus back, the browser fires `focus` again on its active element. No Tab came before it, so `handleFocus` opens the search.
- The merged code now leaves the focus in a closed field in two cases:
  - after Esc or × from 1024 px (`restoreFocus`, quiet: F5 of the NAV review; at 768-1023 px the focus goes to the 🔍, which has no focus handler);
  - after a Tab pass (NAV-11).
- Desktop: switching to another tab and back reopens the dropdown on its own. `html[data-search-open]` and `body.global-search-shown` hide the comparison launcher and the collection selection bar (`_comparison-entry.scss:46`, `_collection-selection.scss:71`), arm the outside-click listener and send `barcode-scan:close`. The dropdown is empty, so the visible change is only the floating bars going away.
- Phones (a hardware keyboard): a Tab pass, then switching apps and back, opens the search view with its own history entry. This is the context change NAV-11 removed for the Tab itself.

**Reproduction.**
1. At 1440 on a collection page with a selection, click the search field and press Esc: the field keeps the focus and the selection bar is shown.
2. Switch to another browser tab and back. `html[data-search-open]` is set again and the selection bar is hidden until the next click outside.

**Fix.**
- In bar mode, ignore a `focus` that only gives the focus back after the window had lost it. For example, on the field's `blur` set `this.leftWithWindow = !document.hasFocus()`; at the top of `handleFocus`, when it is set, clear it and return.
- Alternatively, `focus_origin.js` could export a second marker for this, from a capturing `blur` on `window`.

### F4 · minor · `assets/navigation/focus_origin.js:21`, `tests/topbar-scroll-harness.mjs:124`, `tests/TopbarScrollScriptTest.php:143`
**Problem.** Safari's Option+Tab is not recognized as Tab.
- `installFocusOrigin` ignores a Tab keydown with `altKey`, and the harness pins it (`'altTab' => [false, false]`).
- In Safari (macOS, iPadOS), Option+Tab is the standard key that moves the focus through every element, links and buttons included. Plain Tab reaches only form fields unless "Press Tab to highlight each item" is on.
- WebKit dispatches that keydown to the page (`altKey: true`) before its default action moves the focus. So the field opens on an Option+Tab pass:
  - from 768 px (iPad) it is the dropdown, with the effects listed in F3;
  - on an iPhone with a keyboard it is the search view.
- Excluding Alt protects nothing: on Windows and Linux, Alt+Tab is taken by the system and never arrives as a Tab keydown that moves the focus. Ctrl / Cmd+Tab are excluded correctly.

**Reproduction.** In Safari at 1024 with default settings, press Option+Tab from the logo: the field gets the focus and `html[data-search-open]` is set. Plain Tab, or Chrome, does not open it. In the harness, `afterKey({ key: 'Tab', altKey: true })` returns `[false, false]`.

**Fix.** Drop `event.altKey` from the exclusion (keep Ctrl / Meta) and change the harness row to `altTab: [true, false]`.

## Questions

**Q1 (focus inside the sticky bar while the page is scrolled).**
- The top bar is `position: sticky; top: 0`, and its tools sit inside the band that `scroll-padding-top` now reserves (0-52 / 64 px). The auth header is sticky too.
- When the focus moves into the bar on a scrolled page, the browser's "scroll into view if needed" may treat the target as above the snapport and scroll the page up. The bar stays stuck, so that scroll gains nothing.
  - Desktop: Shift+Tab from `main` passes the column rows (fixed, no scroll), then the avatar, Inbox, ?, + Add, the search field and the logo.
  - Phones: Shift+Tab back to ☰ / Inbox / ?.
  - Possibly also a click into the desktop field.
- Not verified, and it depends on the engine. `checks/NAV-11.py` runs its Tab sequences after `scrollTo({top: 0})` (`:426`), where no scroll is possible.
- Worth a row: at 1440, scroll the Hub to 1500, focus a link in `main`, Shift+Tab until the avatar has focus, and assert that `scrollY` did not change. Do the same at 360 for ☰, and on `/en/login` for the language summary.

**Q2 (scroll padding on pages without bars).** NAV-11 says "`scroll-padding-top/bottom` = bar heights".
- The bar-free pages (`participants_sheet`, `round_stopwatch`, live results entry) render no header. Yet `html`'s `--topbar-h` stays `var(--header-height)`, so `scroll-padding-top` reserves 52 / 64 px that are not there.
  - Live results has its own sticky `.lr-bar` at `top: 0`, which this happens to clear in part.
  - Fix if wanted: `html:not(:has(> body > header.site-header)){scroll-padding-top:0}`. The auth pages keep theirs, because their header is sticky.
- Without `:has()`, the bottom fallback sets `--bottom-nav-height` on `:root` for every phone page. F4's zeros sit on `<body>`, which `html`'s `scroll-padding-bottom` never reads, so the auth, bar-free and multiscan pages reserve 56 px plus the inset there.
- Intended, or should both follow the bars?

**Q3 (upward jumps while the bar is hidden, phones).**
- `scroll-padding-top` reads the live `--topbar-h`: 0 while the bar is hidden.
- An in-page jump or a Shift+Tab to a target above the screen starts with no padding. The scroll up (≥ 8 px) then brings the bar back, over the target: WCAG 2.4.11, the case NAV-11's padding is for. Examples: `#players-directory` after a filter, `.methodology-anchor`, a focused field above.
- This is not a regression: the old margins read the same live value, and focus scrolls had no room at all. The plan prescribes `var(--topbar-h)`.
- With the static `--header-height` on phones, a downward jump would leave a 52 px gap while the bar hides, but nothing would ever be covered. Which one should it be?

**Q4 (200 % at 768-1023 px).**
- `checks/NAV-11.py` checks the 200 % text size at 360, 1024, 1280 and 1440. It never checks the tier with the compact places in the top bar.
- At 200 % (and the 106.25 % root of that tier), the critical CSS (`:120`) gives the compact places:
  - `.nav-places--compact .nav-tab{height:56px}` holding 32 + 2 + a .875rem line (about 30 px), so 64 px, with `white-space:nowrap` labels at about 23 px;
  - next to the "Menu" and "Add" labels at about 30 px, all in 768 px.
- Is that tier covered by "no label clipped", and does it fit?

**Q5 (the phone search view with a hardware keyboard).** The NAV-11 lane made typing the way a keyboard user opens the search view, after a Tab pass. Two things then miss that user:
- Esc closes the layer, and `closeView` blurs the field (`layered`: the on-screen keyboard has to go), so the focus lands on `<body>` (WCAG 2.4.3).
- Tab inside the open view leaves it for the dimmed page behind the scrim. Nothing is `inert`, and `handleFocusOut` returns while `layered`.

Blur only after a tap opening (remember how it opened), and keep the Tab order inside the view? Or is the view deliberately non-modal?

## Checked and fine

**NAV review F1 / Q1 (the bar's state across visits).**
- `topbar_controller` resets `<html>` to `data-topbar="shown"` without `data-topbar-scrolled` in `turbo:before-render` (a morph is skipped). Turbo has merged the head and paints nothing before the body swap, so the new header's first computed style is "shown": no slide-in replay.
- `disconnect()` removes both attributes. Stimulus handles the old body's removal before the new body's addition in the same mutation record, so a page with a bar only flips the attribute between two microtasks.
- On the auth pages, `:root[data-topbar="hidden"] .site-header` no longer matches. The `nav-follow-topbar` rules apply only under `:root[data-topbar]`, so nothing translates there either.
- The first decision waits a frame. `performScroll` runs in the render's microtasks, so the frame reads the new page's position.
- `checks/NAV-07.py` has the visit "puzzle page → sign in (the Profile tab)" starting with the bar hidden.

**NAV review F3 (the scroll clamp).**
- `clampScroll()` bounds `scrollY` to `[0, scrollHeight - innerHeight]`, which is the real maximum on phones (overlay scrollbars).
- A short page (max ≤ 52) never hides. The bounce back from beyond the end is no move, and a real scroll up still shows the bar.
- The harness has all four cases plus the unclamped control.

**NAV review Q2 (rail labels).**
- From 1024 px the rail is `container-type: normal`, so the step container queries find no container.
- `fit()` removes the override first and then, under the desktop query, publishes step 1. A resize back to a phone measures again (ResizeObserver).
- Rail labels wrap (`white-space: normal`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, `hyphens: auto`, centred), and the item is `min-height: 56px`.
- 768-1023: the bar is `display: none`, so `fit()` returns before the query.

**`visit_scroll.js` (instant scroll on visits).**
- It only sets `scroll-behavior: auto` inline on `<html>` (no other code writes that property), from `turbo:before-render` to the next frame after `turbo:render` or to `turbo:load`, whichever comes first. A morph is skipped.
- Turbo scrolls in between: `performScroll` (restored position, anchor with focus, or top), and on the 422 path `scrollToTop`.
- **Restoration visits:** on the web they are full reloads (`app.js`), so the hook is not involved. In the app they are Turbo renders, and their restored position now lands at once.
- **Same-page anchors:** `goToSamePageAnchor` renders nothing, so it stays smooth. The "Jump to me" links are `data-turbo="false"` (native fragments), so they stay smooth too.
- **Explicit calls:** `scrollIntoView({behavior: 'smooth'})` calls are unaffected.
- **Live Components:** they never dispatch Turbo render events.
- **`followRedirect` and the one frame-promoted visit** (`participant_import_preview`, `data-turbo-action="advance"`): Turbo dispatches `turbo:before-render` without a new body. The hold lasts one frame with no scroll, and the bar is shown again; both are harmless.
- `<turbo-stream action="refresh">` (after editing a time and the list selections) is a replace visit, so it behaves like any visit.

**Scroll margins (the 11 stylesheets).**
- The root padding equals the removed `var(--topbar-h)` term, so every converted target lands where it did, and `.lb-row` keeps `--compact-bar-height`.
- Targets inside nested scrollers (`.table-responsive`) gain, because the bar is no longer reserved inside the inner scroller.
- `window.scrollTo` users compute their own offsets and ignore scroll padding: `events_page_controller.scrollToList()`, `auth_disclosure_controller.reveal()` and `live_results` `scrollTo(0, 0)`.
- On short landscape phones (0), in the app (0) and on desktop (64, never hidden) the padding is right.

**`--tabbar-h` growth.**
- `max(56px, calc(39.5px + .875rem))` = 2.5 + 32 + 4 + the label's line + 1 px hairline: 56 px up to a 117 % root font, 68 px at 200 %. Landscape phones at 106.25 % stay at 56.
- Every reader goes through `--bottom-nav-height`: the critical CSS box, `body` padding, the `nav-bottom` mixin and `bottom_sheet_controller`'s `OUT_TRANSFORM`. No JS constant or SCSS rule assumes 56 px for the bar.

**`focus_origin.js`.**
- A capturing `keydown` on `window` marks the task, and `setTimeout(0)` clears it. Focus moves as the default action of that keydown in the same task, so a click can never inherit the mark.
- Encore has one entry with a single runtime, so there is one module instance and the `window.__mspFocusOrigin` guard cannot leave a second instance unmarked.
- Shift+Tab counts, and Ctrl / Cmd+Tab do not.

**The merged search controller.**
- Every line `83dabf56` added is in HEAD.
- A Tab pass followed by typing opens the search (`handleInput`; on phones that is the layer with one entry). A click on the already focused field opens it (`handleFieldClick`).
- The focus put back after Esc or × (`quietFocus`) opens nothing.
- `focusout` ignores:
  - moves inside the instance;
  - moves into a `.modal`;
  - a null `relatedTarget`;
  - phones (`layered`).
- Phones keep the blur on close, desktop keeps the focus. The listeners added in `connect()` and those removed in `disconnect()` match one to one.

**Listeners.**
- `topbar_controller` removes `scroll`, `resize`, the reveal event, `turbo:before-render` and `focusin`, and cancels its frame.
- `bottom_nav_controller` disconnects its ResizeObserver.
- `visit_scroll` and `focus_origin` are installed once per window behind their guards.

**The rest.**
- **Skip link:** a fixed chip at z 1031 (between the bar, 1030, and the selection bar, 1035; the design-system table is updated). It is hidden by the critical `.visually-hidden-focusable` rule otherwise, and its target is `main#main-content`.
- **Contrast:** white on `#d63c42` 4.58:1, `#c8323a` 5.3:1, `#5f6b80` 5.4:1. The button variables use the theme's `cz-` prefix.
- **Icon box:** every glyph fits its box (1.25 / 1.5, 1.0625 / 1.25, place rows 1.1875 / 1.25), and the `.bi` elements are empty, so `overflow: hidden` clips no badge.
- **Footer:** its columns are `minmax(0, 1fr)`, so `overflow-wrap: anywhere` breaks only when the word does not fit.
- **`TurboChromeTest`:** `<html>` carries only `lang` (plus `data-native-app` in the app), no chrome root is `data-turbo-permanent`, and the head flag scripts and the critical CSS are byte-identical across pages and viewers. Turbo re-runs a head script only when its text changes.
- **Turbo contract harness:** it pins the order `before-render:auto` → `render:auto` → `scrollTo:auto` → `load` and the empty property afterwards.
