# Progression launch, October 2026 — decisions and workstreams

Contract for the launch of XP / levels / achievements (PR #128) together with karma, the progression hub and the
weekly digest rework. Owner decisions from 2026-10-10. Drafts (private artifact, owner's account):
"MSP Progression Drafts". Where this file and older docs in `docs/features/xp-levels/` disagree, **this file wins**.

## 1. Decisions (Jan, 2026-10-10)

### Launch
- **All-in launch, no manual feature flag.** The `XP_SYSTEM_ADMIN_ONLY` env flag is removed; `XpFeatureGate` /
  `xp_system_visible()` become the readiness gate below ("ready OR admin"). Problems are fixed in production.
- **Launch-readiness gate instead of a manual flag** (Jan, 2026-10-10): the deploy alone shows nothing new to
  non-admins. The XP backfill, once it completed and verified successfully, writes a "launch ready" marker; from then
  on every surface, the reveal page and the first-visit reveal modal are live for everyone. Before that only admins
  see them (production check). Showing a modal with "Level 1, 1 XP, no achievements" would waste the moment.
- **No separate announcement e-mail.** The XP reveal e-mail (`send-xp-reveal-emails`) is removed. The launch is
  announced inside the regular newsletter (Listmonk, sent ~Sun 11 / Mon 12 Oct 2026) with a universal "Check out your
  level and achievements" link. In the app the launch reveal is offered **once per player** through the announcement
  modal system (`AnnouncementModal`), and from the profile / membership pages.
- **The reveal is a modal on the first visit** (Jan, 2026-10-10): every player with at least one solving time (not
  opted out of the experience system) gets the XP reveal as a modal the first time they enter the site after the
  launch — for those who come before the newsletter or miss it. Once per player; it wins over other announcement
  modals and ignores their 14-day pause; quiet routes still apply. Opening the reveal page
  `/{_locale}/my/xp-reveal` (the newsletter link) counts as having seen the modal.
- **No per-achievement e-mails** (already decided 2026-10-04): `badges_earned` mail is removed; achievements reach
  players in the app and in the activity digest.
- **Formula cutoff = the deploy moment** (decided 2026-10-04): stored once by a migration, not a code constant.
- ~~The level curve stays (Level 50 = 3,160 XP).~~ Superseded 2026-10-11 by "XP balance" below. Level 50 must be
  rare, not reachable in one year of puzzling.

### XP balance (Jan, 2026-10-11, while testing before the launch)
Measured on the production copy by replaying the last 12 months of real results (5,865 active players):
- **The incentives made levels come 1.7× faster than the curve was calibrated for.** The v4 curve was tuned on the
  history formula (no incentives); new solves would have earned +50 % weekly boost on the first 5 solves of a week and a
  flat +2 daily warm-up, i.e. +39 % and +31 % of the base XP (the median puzzler, 14 solves a year, nearly every solve
  got both): median 60 → 114 XP a year, Level 50 after a year 249 → 374 players, from zero 5 → 25.
  → **Weekly boost +20 %, no daily warm-up** (Jan picked it from: as built / +25 % & +1 / +20 % & none / no incentives).
- **"Faster than the median, no bonus" (a player's report):** +5 % of a 500-piece core (5-6 XP) is 0.25-0.3 and was
  rounded to 0; on 500 pieces (76 % of solo timed solves) all three speed tiers paid 0 or 1 XP, the +20 % boost was 0 on
  puzzles of 200 pieces or less, the difficulty bonus 0 on small hard puzzles. → **Every bonus the solve qualifies for
  is worth at least 1 XP** (difficulty, unboxed, speed, weekly boost; the history formula too), so every promised line
  shows up on the receipt.
- **Curve v5: Level 50 after about 1,000 logged puzzles including the bonuses** (Jan: "think about max level to be
  reachable after about 1000 solved puzzle"). Under the final formula the 44 players with 1,000 XP-earning solves held a
  median 5,793 XP at their 1,000th (p25 5,098, p75 6,627; 5.96 XP per solve on average, history formula 4.58) →
  **Level 50 = 5,800 XP**. A uniform stretch (×1.835) would have left a first 500-piece solve at Level 1, so the shape is
  v4 × (1 + 0.835 × ((L − 1) / 49)^1.5): Level 2 = 5 and Level 5 = 22 as before, the stretch grows towards the top, no
  step smaller than the one before. Projection: 18 players at Level 50 at launch (v4: 112), median Level 10 (unchanged),
  90 at Level 50 a year on, 2 reaching it from zero within a year; a newcomer is Level 9 after ~10 solves, Level 27
  after ~100. History stays deliberately smaller than new solves (the formula cutoff), which is why fewer than the 44
  are Level 50 at launch.

### Currencies — each is fed by one kind of activity only
| Family | What | Fed by | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Progress | XP + Level 1–50 | logged puzzles only | everyone, free |
| Collection | Achievements + Achievement Points (AP) | achievement tiers | **members only** |
| Competition | MSP Rating (becomes seasonal in its own project) + Skill tier (stays) | times | as today |
| Community | **Karma** | accepted contributions to the database | everyone, free, public list |

- **Achievements grant no XP** any more. AP only. Nothing converts into anything else.
- **Karma grants no XP.**
- **Non-members get no achievements.** They are teased without revealing which: "You earned an achievement with this
  time. Become a member to find out which." (The model: esports platforms that show "you would have earned …" behind
  the subscription.) Never "you will lose them".
- A **public XP leaderboard is fine** ("nearly everything we do is public and transparent").

### Karma (v1)
- Name: **Karma**. Purely informational credit: precise, public, shows who helps the database. Nothing to spend.
- **1 karma per accepted contribution. No caps, no weights.**
  - an approved change request (proposer)
  - an approved merge request (reporter)
  - a new puzzle **with an EAN** that is approved (adder) — **unless it turns out to be a duplicate**: when puzzles are
    merged, only the person who added the puzzle first keeps the credit (the later copy was not a positive
    contribution). The merge reporter gets their own 1.
  - a moderator decision (approve or reject a change request, merge request, puzzle approval) — 1 each
  - a moderator's direct puzzle edit — 1
  - a moderator deciding their own proposal — 1 in total (not 2)
- Declined work never takes karma away. No negative karma. No events in v1. Admins are excluded (internal-API
  proposals are credited to the admin reviewer player).
- **Derived from existing records** (change/merge requests, puzzles, `puzzle_merge_audit`, `puzzle_moderation_decision`),
  denormalized to `player.karma` for lists; a backfill/recalculation command; the same derivation answers
  "what did I get my karma for?" on a karma history page.
- Shown: profile chip (when > 0), public karma list, karma history (own), admin (player pages + review queues show the
  proposer's karma and acceptance rate).

### Digest ("activity digest" e-mail)
- **Paused until the week after the newsletter.** The first activity digest goes out **Monday 19 Oct 2026** about
  12–18 Oct. Nothing is sent for earlier periods.
- Sent on **Monday** to everyone who had **any activity** in the period.
- **No digest without activity.** A player with no activity in their period gets no digest. **"We miss you" mail**
  (Jan, 2026-10-11): after **4 weeks without activity**, once, never again until they have had activity again — very
  friendly, lightly funny ("have you been puzzling and forgot to track your times?"), encouraging to puzzle and track.
  Only players with at least one result ever. Weekly Tuesday run, first one **Tue 27 Oct 2026** (two weeks after the newsletter; Jan, 2026-10-11), in recency
  waves (27 Oct: 28d-3m + visitors with an older result; 3 Nov: 3-9m; weekly cron from 10 Nov); never for dormant
  accounts (no result and no visit for more than 9 months);
  a 10 % holdout is logged, not mailed, to measure the comebacks.
  Built (WS-E): last logged result ≥ 28 days ago, once per inactive spell (the clock restarts with any result), rides
  on the digest switch and its unsubscribe, paced like the digest with a stricter breaker, `--dry-run` and
  `--max-recipients` for the first wave of dormant accounts.
- **Frequency setting**: weekly (default) / every two weeks / monthly / off. Own switch + own one-click unsubscribe,
  separate from the unread-messages digest (decided 2026-10-04).
- Pacing over the day + circuit breaker (decided 2026-10-04, `docs/features/xp-levels/README.md`).
- The mail's main button opens a **dedicated "your week" page** — the period's activity on the site, every line of the
  mail links somewhere meaningful.

### Auditability and performance
- Every number must be explainable by the player: "where did my XP come from?" (XP history, grouped by solve),
  "what did I get my karma for?" (karma history), achievements (requirement + progress per tier).
- Performance matters: no query per page for the header (values ride on the viewer's `PlayerProfile` row), one
  aggregate statement per list, query-count tests for new pages.

### UI (from the drafts)
- **"How it works" hub**: grouped list (draft A) — groups by concept: "Just you" (Level & XP, Achievements),
  "Against the clock" (MSP Rating, Skill tier), "For everyone" (Karma). Each card → its deep page. Linked from every
  "?" next to a progression chip, the footer "Learn" column and the user menu.
- **After logging a time**: one progress card (level line, place on the puzzle, achievement line, rating/tier line),
  **plus real attention for milestones**: an earned achievement gets a prominent block with "tap to reveal" (the
  last-piece puzzle reveal); a level-up gets a lighter celebration (ring fills, "Level 13!"), bigger only for milestone
  levels. Nothing replays on reload.
- **Profile header**: draft A — the level ring around the header avatar (no second avatar), one row of ≤ 3 chips
  (skill tier, MSP Rating place, karma when > 0), achievements showcase near the top with the AP total (members).
- **Navbar**: the user icon becomes the player's avatar, or their initial on a coloured disc when there is no avatar
  (same as leaderboard / recent activity rows).
- Names: "Achievements" in copy ("badge" only means the picture), "Achievement Points (AP)", "MSP Rating", "Skill tier"
  (one spelling), positions as "#3 of 40", "Leaderboards" for the boards, "Karma".

## 3. Workstream D — what shipped (hub, pages, fixes)

- **"How progress works"** `progression_hub` (`/en/how-progress-works`, public, static): draft A. Cards to gated deep
  pages (`xp_explainer`, `badges_overview#how-achievements-work`, `karma_explainer`) are plain text until
  `xp_system_visible()`; MSP Rating / Skill tier link `methodology#msp-rating` / `#skill-tier`. Linked from the user
  menu (with "My achievements" and "My XP history"), the footer's Learn column and the methodology page - all gated.
  The sitemap lists the progression pages once the gate is open for guests (`SitemapStaticController::PROGRESSION_ROUTES`).
- **Achievements catalogue** `badges_overview`: rows, `?show=next|earned|all|rarest` (`AchievementCatalogueFilter`;
  next/earned for members only), rarity per tier from one aggregate (`GetAchievementRarity` →
  `AchievementRarityProvider`, pool `achievement_rarity_cache`, 15 min; puzzlers = players with XP). Non-members get
  the catalogue built without a player - nothing personal is read - plus a tease line. Info modal = tier rows
  (tier · requirement · % of puzzlers · ✓/progress). AP chip `.ap-chip` (amber), never `.xp-level-chip`.
- **Holders** `achievement_detail`: 44 px pills (`_player_avatar`), country names (`CountryFilterOptions`),
  `auto_submit_controller.js` instead of inline `onchange`, hero = the viewer's tier (members) or Bronze.
- **Leaderboards**: no promo banners on `/ladder`; `_ladder_dropdown` lists the MSP Rating and the progression boards
  (`ProgressLeaderboardTab`). XP/AP board on the shared `lb-table` row, level as secondary text, the viewer's row pinned
  under the list on the filtered board (`GetXpLeaderboard::selfRank()` takes country + favorites, ties like the list);
  the favorites board includes the viewer. **A new board** (karma) = a `ProgressLeaderboardTab` case, a `rows()` arm in
  `ProgressLeaderboardController` (+ its own route: each board is its own page since 2026-10-11), its column in `selfRank()`, `xp.leaderboard.board.<tab>.*` translations.
- **XP history**: `GetXpHistory::blocksForPlayer()` - one block per solve (settlements and deletion included), newest
  change first, 20 per page, two statements per page.
- **Puzzle page estimate**: base (+ difficulty for members only), repeat discount in the number, no bonus number.
