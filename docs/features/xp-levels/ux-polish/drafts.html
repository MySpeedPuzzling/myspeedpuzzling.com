Five ways to make progress, and how to show them without the noise

The progression system now reaches 74 places on the site, e-mails and API. These drafts show how XP, achievements, karma, the MSP Rating and skill tiers can sit together, one clear home each, on a 375 px phone first.

Built from three studies done today: an inventory of every progression touch point on PR #128, a karma simulation on the dev database (a production copy from 25 Sept), and published research on gamification. Drafts are static mock-ups with made-up names and realistic numbers.

01

Five concepts, four families

Players can hold about four ideas at once. The MSP Rating and the skill tier both measure speed (this season, and all time), so they belong to one family. That leaves four questions a player can ask, each with one colour and one icon used everywhere. Each family is fed by one kind of activity only: nothing converts into anything else.

How much have I puzzled?
Progress
XP and Level 1–50. Only from logged puzzles. Never competes. Free.
What have I done?
Collection
Achievements Bronze → Diamond and Achievement Points. For hunters. AP shown to members.
How fast am I?
Competition
MSP Rating (seasonal, coming separately) and Skill tier Enthusiast → Legend (all-time).
How have I helped?
Community
Karma. 1 per accepted contribution to the database. Public, free, gives no XP.

Rules that keep it calm

  • Each concept has one home; everywhere else it is at most a small chip that links home.
  • Never all five on one card. Player cards get Level + skill tier; the profile gets the rest.
  • The metal names Bronze → Diamond belong to achievements only.
  • Every chip's "?" opens the matching card of the "How it works" hub.

No more XP from achievements? recommended

Today every achievement tier also pays its AP value in XP (5/10/25/50/100). Dropping that makes the rule "XP = puzzling, AP = achievements, karma = helping" true without exceptions.

  • Free players earn XP from achievements they cannot see today. That goes away.
  • The launch deck shows "+AP" per badge instead of "+XP"; the counter is puzzle XP from the start.
  • Delete XpReason::Achievement entries (a recompute already exists), drop the grant in BadgeEvaluator.
  • Check the level curve: Level 50 = 3,160 XP was tuned with achievement XP in it. Without it veterans reach levels later; re-run the curve on the dev data before deciding the thresholds.

What exists in code today

  • XP / Level: built on PR #128, behind the xp-system flag.
  • Achievements: 16 tiered + Early Adopter, AP in player.achievement_points.
  • Competition = the MSP Rating (500 pc, becomes seasonal in its own project) + the skill tier (stays). Out of scope here except for placement.
  • Karma: not built. Librarian and Cataloger achievements already reward the same work.

36 % of last year's active puzzlers would hold karma; median 3. 59 % of new puzzles carry an EAN, so the EAN rule trims puzzle karma by about 44 % without making it rare.

95 % of all karma would come from adding puzzles (22,589 of 23,841). Fixes and merges are ~1,250 points across ~250 people, and would be invisible in one total. The drafts therefore always show karma with its breakdown, and the public list can be filtered by kind (draft). Moderator decisions are not in this dev copy (25 Sept).

A puzzle that turns out to be a duplicate

When two puzzles are merged later, both adders got 1 karma for "the same" puzzle. Proposal: the first one to add it keeps the credit.

  • Because karma is calculated from the records, this happens by itself on the next recalculation: the later copy stops counting, the earlier one keeps its point, and whoever reported the duplicate gets 1.
  • Who added a merged-away puzzle is known from puzzle_merge_audit (since 16 Sept). Older merges: the surviving puzzle's adder keeps it.
  • No notice and no minus line. The karma history shows "merged into a puzzle added earlier" in grey, so the drop is explained if someone looks.
  • Accepted change proposals on a puzzle that is later merged keep their karma (they move with the merge and were real work).

Calculated, not collected

  • Derived from change and merge requests, puzzles, the merge audit and the append-only decision log, then stored on player.karma for lists, like AP.
  • A backfill command fills it once; the same command repairs drift. A daily run is a safety net; accepted decisions update the two people involved at once.
  • Proposals filed through the internal API are credited to the admin player today: admins are left out of karma and its list.

Karma has a second job: moderators see the proposer's karma and acceptance rate next to each request (draft). With ~670 suggestions waiting, that is where it saves the most time.

Free vs member

AP stay members-only. One thing worth considering: research finds that gamification does not convert by itself, and that what converts is people seeing what they have already earned (paywalled status drew backlash at Strava in 2020).

  • Option: free players see their own earned achievements (name and tier, privately), without AP, art reveals or the public showcase.
  • Copy: "You have earned 7 achievements. Members see their badges, Achievement Points and show them on their profile." Never "you will lose them".

Open: see D3.

12

Rollout

Gamification is hard to take back: Nike Run Club's App Store rating fell from 4.5 to 1.5 when trophies were removed. Ship what we intend to keep, one family at a time, and introduce each concept when it first means something to the player.

StepShipsA player first meets it
1Renames, fixes above, the hub, recap card, profile header, achievements without XP—
2Level + Achievements (PR #128 launch reveal)launch reveal, then the first logged time
3Karma + backfill + public list + review-queue contextthe first accepted puzzle or suggestion
4MSP Rating seasons (own project)the first ranked result of a season

Measure each step: D7/D30 retention of new players, times logged per active player, free → trial → paid. Published effects of badges are 0–2 %; a holdout tells us more than intuition.

13

Decisions

Decided by Jan, 10 Oct

  • Karma, under that name: 1 per accepted contribution, no caps. New puzzles count when they have an EAN. Moderators earn 1 per decision and per direct edit; a proposal they decide themselves counts once.
  • Karma is free, public (with a list), calculated from existing records with a backfill command, gives no XP. No events in v1.
  • MSP Rating becomes seasonal in its own project; skill tiers stay as they are.

Achievements stop granting XP?

Recommended: yes, so XP comes from puzzling only. Needs a re-check of the level curve on the dev data first (Level 50 = 3,160 XP was tuned with achievement XP in).

Duplicates merged later

Recommended: the first one to add the puzzle keeps the credit, computed automatically; no notice, a grey line in the karma history.

Free players and their own achievements

Keep members-only, or let free players see their own earned achievements by name (no AP, no art, no showcase).

XP leaderboard default

Recommended: keep the tab, default to people you follow. Alternatives: world (as built), or no public XP board.

Recap and profile layouts

Recap A (one progress card) and profile A (ring + status row), or the B variants.

Hub layout

A (grouped list, recommended) or B (2 × 2 grid with your own values).
